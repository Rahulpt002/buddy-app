Concept 15: Diorama a cozy isometric room

Your desk drawn as a little isometric room where every object is live and tappable: the monitor graphs your CPU, the laptop shows Claude usage, the printer fills up layer by layer, the window shows the weather, the wall calendar lists today, the clock tells the time and the plant grows a leaf per day of GitHub streak. Each detail page zooms into that object next to soft cards. Light and dark themes: tap the theme button in the top bar to switch, or leave it on Auto to follow your phone.
