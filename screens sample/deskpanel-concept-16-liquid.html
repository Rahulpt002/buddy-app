Concept 16: Liquid a Liquid Glass style

Everything floats as translucent glass over a living wallpaper that takes its color from what is on screen, and on Music it is the album art itself. Glass has specular rims and bends the content at its edges (in Chrome and Android WebView; other browsers show plain frosted glass). The tab bar floats, with a droplet that slides between tabs; shortcuts are laid out like Control Center. Switch Clear or Tinted glass in the top bar.
