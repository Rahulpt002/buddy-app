Concept 6: Cockpit a glass-cockpit display

Borrowed from aircraft displays, which are designed to be read at a glance. Pages sit on bezel soft-keys along the top and bottom edges, values live on dials with caution zones, and colors follow cockpit rules: green is normal, cyan is selected, magenta is a target or the next thing, amber is caution.
